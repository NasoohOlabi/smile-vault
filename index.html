About Us

Dama is an independent creative platform observing Damascus through the lens of urban life, culture, and everyday experience. We tell stories, document visual and cultural details, and create designs and projects that invite people to look at familiar places and topics differently.

Imagine it this way: an observatory overlooking Damascus from Mount Qassioun, with a shop on the first floor selling graphic t-shirts and prints. The designs are based on what this observatory has noticed and learned, presented artfully and sarcastically to accompany you some days a week. We prioritise growing through collabs with other creatives and community support, along with some side gigs!

Since I came up with the idea around 24 July 2024, Dama has lived through my Architect — parametric design career, then the shift to Architect — experience design, then to creative production / project management. Plus, all the side gigs and voluntary work I do. So, what I am presenting to you is what I fought to have the time to finish and put up, piece by piece, and day by day. From my mind and soul to yours…

- Sham
